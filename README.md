## What I'm building

**[Neurvane Focus](https://neurvane.com)** — a deep-work Chrome extension for blocking distractions, scheduling focus sessions, tracking local productivity stats, and using optional bring-your-own-key AI coaching. The product is designed around one principle: protect attention without stealing user data.

**DistroAI** — B2B SaaS in development for FMCG distributors. It turns messy WhatsApp and email orders into structured SKUs, clean invoices, and demand signals that can later power forecasting and inventory decisions.

## Featured project

**[TwinCar](https://github.com/Hristijan-kiko/twincar)** — vehicle make-and-model recognition system; my Data Science & AI Academy capstone and the cohort-winning project.

- EfficientNet-B3 classifier fine-tuned on Stanford Cars
- YOLO-based crop step to clean vehicle inputs before classification
- 78.64% fine-grained top-1 validation accuracy on clean data
- 95.2% top-5 validation accuracy
- Robustness testing across clean, augmented, and hard samples
- Experiment tracking with Weights & Biases
- FastAPI inference, Gradio demo, Hugging Face deployment, and CI workflow
