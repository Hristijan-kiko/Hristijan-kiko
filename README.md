## What I'm building

**[Neurvane Focus](https://neurvane.com)** — a Chrome extension for deep work. Distraction blocking with a bring-your-own-key AI coach across seven LLM providers, on-device focus stats, and a hard mode that actually holds. Your keys and your data never leave your machine.

**DistroAI** — B2B SaaS that automates the back office for FMCG distributors. It turns messy WhatsApp and email orders into parsed SKUs and clean invoices, with demand forecasting layered on top.
